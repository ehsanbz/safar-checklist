Rain / cancellation Plan B

Holbox

Move Punta Mosquito/beach time if possible; use town, cafés and rest as backup. Recheck ferry operations separately.

Valladolid

Spend more time in the historic centre, Casa de los Venados and cafés; postpone swimming if thunderstorms make it unsafe.

Mérida / Uxmal

If Uxmal is impractical, use museums, Casa de Montejo, markets and central Mérida instead.

Bacalar

If the boat tour is cancelled, use Fuerte de San Felipe and the town as backup; stay out of the water in thunderstorms.

Tulum

Shorten or move the ruins visit in heavy rain and continue to Playa del Carmen earlier.

Playa del Carmen

If an outdoor activity is cancelled, use cafés, food, shopping and rest as a simple fallback.
